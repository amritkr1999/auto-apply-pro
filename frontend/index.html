EasyNaukri

Automated Job Application & Resume Matching Platform

EasyNaukri is an AI-powered career assistant and job outreach automation platform designed to simplify the job search process for candidates. EasyNaukri helps job seekers match their resumes with job descriptions, optimize ATS keywords, track job application pipelines, and manage recruiter outreach.

Google Authentication & Integration

EasyNaukri integrates with Google OAuth to allow users to securely sign in to their EasyNaukri account profile. Additionally, EasyNaukri utilizes Google Sign-In and Gmail API scopes (send-only access) so users can compose, review, and send candidate-initiated job application outreach emails directly to recruiters from their connected Gmail account.
